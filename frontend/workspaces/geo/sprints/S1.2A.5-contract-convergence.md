### S3-01: Contract Check Script
Create `workspaces/geo/scripts/check-api-contract.mjs` that:
1. Reads `API_CONTRACT.yaml`
2. Extracts all `canonical: true` and `deprecated: true` endpoints
3. Scans backend route files for matching `app.get/post('/api/geo/{path}'`
